Scope

This CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA privacy policy governs the collection, use and other processing of personal data provided by Users on this website or on any of the CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA online environments.

By using the forms included on the various websites, Users accept the inclusion and processing of the data they provide in a personal data processing operation for which CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA is the controller, and may exercise the relevant rights.

Who is the data controller?

CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA is responsible for collecting and processing your personal data in connection with the services it provides, and undertakes to respect and safeguard your privacy.

Controller: Ferran Escardó (CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA) · Tax ID: 35085389Q · Address: Carrer Plutó, 24, 08035 Barcelona, Spain · Contact: info@carquinyolisdhorta.com.

Why do we collect your data?

In accordance with current legislation, CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA collects only the data strictly necessary to provide the services arising from its activity. This website processes only the contact details provided by Users, for the purposes of: managing contact details, sending information that may be of interest, customer management and prospective customer management.

No user profiling is carried out. The information may be used to produce statistical studies of registered Users.

How long do we keep your data?

Personal data will be kept until the User states otherwise and for the retention periods established by law, unless for clear and obvious reasons it has lost its usefulness or the legitimate purpose for which it was collected.

Who will your data be shared with?

Personal data entered on the website will not be disclosed to third parties, except in the cases established by current legislation. CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA does not sell, rent or transfer Users' personal data or email addresses, except where necessary to provide the service itself.

What rights do Users have?

Users may exercise the rights recognised in Regulation (EU) 2016/679, in particular the rights to portability, access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, by sending a signed written request together with a copy of an identity document or passport to: CARQUINYOLIS D'HORTA, Carrer Plutó, 24, 08035 Barcelona, Spain.

Users may also withdraw their consent at any time and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

User commitment and responsibility

Entering data in any of the collection forms implies acceptance of the terms of use and the privacy policy. The User is responsible for the authenticity of the data provided, which must be accurate, current and complete. Where the data belongs to a third party, the User is solely responsible for having properly obtained that party's consent and for having informed them.
