What cookies are and what they do

In accordance with Spanish Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce, we inform you that this website uses cookies in order to provide a better service and a better browsing experience.

Cookies are small text files containing information that are created on the User's computer or device when browsing the website. By browsing this website you consent to receiving these cookies.

What types of cookies do we use?

By duration: session cookies (which remain until you leave the page) and persistent cookies (whose data stays stored for a set period).

By purpose: first-party and third-party cookies; technical cookies (necessary for browsing and proper functioning); analytics cookies (measurement and statistical analysis of website use); and advertising and behavioural advertising cookies.

How can I manage or disable cookies?

You can set your browser to notify you when cookies are received and to refuse them. Disabling cookies may, however, limit or prevent access to some website features.

Further information is available at Aboutcookies, youronlinechoices, the Network Advertising Initiative or the Digital Advertising Alliance.

Third-party cookies

Google: when you visit the website, cookies may be sent from domains such as www.google.com and www.doubleclick.net. Google Analytics is the analytics tool that helps us understand how users interact with the website, gathering statistical usage information without personally identifying visitors.
