Some flavours should never disappear.

The tradition is still alive

Things done well take time

We work with 100% artisan butter and with hazelnuts and almonds from Catalonia. The recipe draws on a crunchier, more moreish carquinyoli than the usual ones.

They are cut one by one before they go into the oven. That way they don't come out slice-shaped like the more common version: it is what sets them apart, and it is how it should be done.

Every morning begins the same way.
And we love that.

The difference is in the details

Three ways of understanding craft

Ingredients

Hazelnuts and almonds from Catalonia, artisan butter and organic lemon zest. Local produce of the highest quality.

Method

Cut one by one. No rush, with the same care as always.

Tradition

A way of working rooted in the Horta neighbourhood, kept alive because someone decided to protect it.

We don't speed time up. Because good flavour was never in a hurry.
