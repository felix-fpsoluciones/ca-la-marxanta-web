The workshop

Things done well take time

We're not trying to make more. We're trying to make it better.

The handmade process

Six steps

01

Selection

We choose hazelnuts and almonds from Catalonia, artisan butter and organic lemon zest.

02

Mixing

We work the ingredients in exactly the right proportions, with no unnecessary additives.

03

By hand

We shape the dough by hand, with the same care as always.

04

Cut one by one

We cut each carquinyoli one by one before baking. That is why they don't come out slice-shaped.

05

Baking

The bake is what gives them their characteristic crunch.

06

Packing

We pack them carefully so they reach your home in perfect condition.

We believe in time, in hands, and in respect for a recipe.

Our ingredients

Good ingredients never need hiding

Almond and hazelnut

From Catalonia. They give our carquinyolis their deep, distinctive flavour.

Artisan butter

100% artisan: the heart of the Petarrons and their unmistakable texture.

Organic lemon zest

A fresh, aromatic note, present in almost every recipe.

Egg

It binds the dough and gives it structure; in the Petarrons we use the white.

Brown sugar

For a measured sweetness, never excessive.

Wheat flour

The base of the whole family, worked with time and patience.
