Our story

The best stories begin with a decision

The decision to do things the way they have always been done: with time, with care, and with real ingredients.

Traditions don't survive by chance. They survive because someone decides to protect them.

The name

Why "Ca la Marxanta"?

A marxanta is someone who goes to market, who travels the roads carrying good produce from one place to another. The name evokes that old, local trade: today our carquinyolis travel from the Horta workshop to tables across Catalonia.

The Horta neighbourhood

I'm from Horta

Horta is one of the most characterful neighbourhoods in Barcelona, and it has always held on to its identity. You will even see people walking proudly in T-shirts that read "Sóc d'Horta" — I'm from Horta. Sometimes it doesn't feel like Barcelona at all: the atmosphere is quite different.
